What we measure, and what we won't say

SnapGut looks for associations: foods that show up in the hours before you feel worse, more often than your own baseline would explain — matched across windows from 30 minutes to 48 hours.

A food is only ranked once your log holds enough of it to say anything. Below that bar you see the pattern and its confidence, not a verdict — and no opaque 0–100 score anywhere.

What that is not: SnapGut is not a medical device, it does not diagnose anything, and it is not an allergy or intolerance test. A pattern in your log is a place to look — not proof that one food caused one symptom.

Your data, your device

No profile, no quiz — just an email. Your log lives in your browser's own storage. Meal photos are sent only to identify the meal, and we don't keep them.

You choose where it syncs

  • Local only. The default. The only thing sent anywhere is a photo being identified.
  • A Google Sheet you own. Your log, mirrored into your own spreadsheet. A Pro feature; photos are never written to it.
  • SnapGut Cloud. Cross-device sync, opt-in, a Pro feature. Your device stays the source of truth.

Switch destinations or turn them off at any time. Export everything to CSV or JSON, free, whenever you want — your data is yours, so keep a copy wherever you like.
