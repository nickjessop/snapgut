Questions people ask first

Do I have to pay to keep my own log?

No. Your log lives on your device, and manual logging, the statistics, and export stay free whether or not you ever buy Pro.

What does the free AI trial cover?

Photo recognition and written insights share one trial allowance on a new account. When it runs out, logging carries on exactly as before — you type the ingredients instead of photographing them.

What does SnapGut tell me?

Which foods tend to show up before the hours you felt worse, compared with meals without them. That is an association, not a diagnosis, and it is not an allergy or intolerance test.

What happens to my meal photos?

A photo is sent to identify the meal and is not kept afterwards. Your log stays on your device unless you turn on a sync destination yourself.
